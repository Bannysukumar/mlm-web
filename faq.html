General Questions

MLM Platform is a multi-level marketing platform that offers investment packages and various bonus opportunities. Our platform helps individuals achieve financial success through a proven system of investments and referrals.

Getting started is easy! Simply register an account, choose an investment package, and begin earning through our various bonus systems. You can also invite others to join using your unique referral code.

Investment & Packages

We offer various investment packages ranging from basic to premium levels. Each package has different investment amounts and corresponding daily bonus rates. Visit our Opportunity page to view all available packages.

Daily bonuses are calculated based on your package investment amount and the corresponding bonus rate. These bonuses are credited to your account daily and can be withdrawn once you meet the minimum withdrawal requirements.

Referral System

When someone joins using your referral code, they become part of your downline. You earn referral bonuses based on their package investments and can earn up to 5 levels deep in the referral chain.

Referral bonus rates vary by level. Level 1 (direct referrals) typically earns the highest rate, with decreasing rates for levels 2-5. Specific rates are detailed in our bonus structure documentation.

Withdrawals & Payments

To withdraw funds, you need a minimum balance of $100, at least 2 direct referrals, and an active package. Withdrawals are processed on specific days and are subject to a service fee.

We support various payment methods including bank transfers, cryptocurrency, and USDT (BEP20/TRC20). Each method has its own processing time and requirements.
